Method

Each parcel is joined to its zone, SDAT record and building footprints. Parcels that are not single-family detached, or are in a zone that does not allow a detached ADU, are marked ineligible. For the rest, the zone's accessory-structure setbacks are cut back from the lot line, the house footprint plus a separation buffer is removed, and the largest rectangle that fits in what is left (at several rotations) is measured. A lot fits when that rectangle meets the minimum dimension and area, and the maximum floor area is capped by lot coverage and the ADU size cap.

Notes and limits

  • ADUs are modelled as 1-story only (max_stories: 1).
  • In RE and R zones an ADU must sit behind the rear wall of the house; the screen enforces this.
  • Lots with street frontage on both front and back get the front setback from both street frontages. Many of these probably back onto alleys, so those lots may be undercounted.
  • The corner-lot rear setback is applied to all corner lots (conservative; the code applies it only where the abutting lot fronts the side street).
  • The R-60/R-90 rule adding +1 ft of setback per foot of ADU length beyond 24 ft along a lot line is not modelled. It does not change which lots fit, but it overstates usable_footprint_sqft, max_adu_floor_area and the CSV ranking for large ADUs.
  • corner_lot_front: shorter is a heuristic.
  • Building footprints may predate recent construction, so a few lots may differ on the ground.
  • Pilot verification against aerial imagery passed at 40/40 on 2026-11-09.
  • Not modelled: tree canopy, slope, easements, floodplain or stream buffers, HOA covenants, utility capacity, existing or licensed ADUs, attached or interior ADUs, cost.
